Work Text:

There was a before this moment.

There was an after this moment. 

Riza found herself trapped on the tightrope of that moment. 

Test dummies and targets were one thing. A splinter of wood here, the puff of stuffing there. It felt like a game, the way instructors barked orders and praise in the same breath. The debris from a good shot had been a badge of honor. 

The spray off this shot. Her first. Her true first—it wasn’t like the practice at all. 

Even as an experienced hunter, there was something grotesquely different about the experience. She’d heard people equate the two before. You’re not hunting deer and squirrels, it’s traitors. This didn’t feel like a traitor. This felt like a person. It did. 

The distance between her trigger and that body. The speed of the bullet wasn’t fast enough to beat the dissolution of her own sense of everything about the person she thought she was in that split second. 

Before:

She was a hero. She was everything that they promised in the academy. She’d served her country with honor, and she’d made enough money to finally, finally live instead of ration day to day. 

In the before, her thoughts about after were something more glowing and golden. Even with the tattoo marring her back and its secrets, she’d help defeat an evil that might use that. She’d go home—or, she’d make a home for the first time in her life. She’d find love. 

The pudgy hands and faces in this crystalized, fractured future reflected something of her father’s former student, but that was neither here nor there. 

She would make the future what she wanted. That was what the naivete of the before promised. Warmth. Children’s toys. Laughter. It flowed in abundance. It was something she’d never heard as a child, but these metaphorical children would have everything so much better than she had. 

How could they not?

Their mother saved lives. She followed her training, rose to the top. Her superiors had recognized something in her that no one had before. 

Then the moment. 

A moment she’d trap herself in.

Inescapeable. Choking. Drowning in the blood that sprayed miles upon miles away but seemed to creep up her throat. Or maybe it bile. It was something that made her retch. It boiled black inside her. Not a dummy. Not a target. 

A life. 

A splatter of so much blood that then became the pool she would drown herself in. Impossibly red, even from a perch. Even in a boiling sun. 

It all boiled. It seared and blackened. Her soul shrivelled within it. 

The moment dawned with the same red of a new day, and all her future held would now be stained in that sticky crimson. 

It wasn’t a saving grace. It was her death linked to a body whose name she’d never know. It was a betrayal.

Betrayal of what? 

Her morals. Her future. Who she thought she’d be. The children she’d never have who now pointed their imaginary fingers towards her: Murderer. Murderer. Murderer. 

After: 

Failure. 

Not simply the system that had failed her or every decision she’d made and had been made for her before then. It was a failure to recognize what this was in the first place.

Murder. 

There was now only an after. 

Murder.

Never again would her hands be clean of it. Never would her soul feel the burden lifted. Nothing could ever remove this mark from her. 

Murderer. 

Her mind no longer belonged to the body it once occupied, or maybe it was the other way around. In any case, she wasn’t who she was before. She was left with only two truths. 

Your oaths poison those you would protect.

You are a murderer.
